Our garden

A garden first, a venue second

Swan Creek is a family-run garden in the Chishawasha Hills, and it still feels like one. Tall trees for shade, green lawns, and a stone path through the arch to where you'll say your vows. We look after it all year so it's ready for your day.

Ceremonies under the msasa trees. Lawns for photos and for the children to run on. We terrace the gentle slope into photo spots, so every corner is ready for a picture.

  • 30 minutes from Harare
  • Golden-hour finish by 6pm

The Gazebo · since 2018

One room, dressed for you

Our permanent thatched reception space, so there's no marquee to hire. We dress it differently for every couple: white and gold, navy and blue, blush and burgundy. Lighting, florals, the dance floor and the head table are all styled to your colours.

Everything you need, nothing you don't. More of your budget goes on the celebration.
